Privacy Policy — hermes agents

hermes agents is used only by its owner with the owner's own Google account.

Data accessed. The app reads and writes the owner's Google Drive files, Calendar events, Docs, Sheets and Gmail messages, as granted on the Google consent screen.

Use of data. Data is used only to provide the owner's personal automation: backups, note sync, calendar and mail summaries. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing. Data is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

Storage. Data stays on the owner's own server and in the owner's Google account. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
